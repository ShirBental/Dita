## What it does
- **Runtime path:** synthetic Telegram replay → real poller and adapter → ingestion (SYNTHETIC, `tgr` namespace) → enqueue hook → `/extract` by a configured approver → collect and approve (E-1) → worker → Muse client over the RECORDED transport → evidence validator → stored knowledge → outbox relay → Vespa and Neo4j → knowledge retrieval → grounded, cited `/ask`.
- **No shortcuts:** the test never ingests, enqueues, approves or inserts knowledge directly. The only test-side writes are one negative-control batch, which must stay unprocessed.
